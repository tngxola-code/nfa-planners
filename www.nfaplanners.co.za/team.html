ABOUT MONGEZI

Mongezi Ngxola is a seasoned Professional Planner with a Master's Degree in Urban and Regional Planning. Registered with the South African Council for Planners, he brings over two decades of experience in Land Survey, spanning private sector, Eskom, and entrepreneurial ventures.
A visionary leader, Mongezi co-founded NFA Town and Regional Planners in 2016, where he serves as a Director. His expertise encompasses a broad range of planning and surveying services, informed by his extensive experience and passion for innovative spatial solutions.

ABOUT FEZILE

Fezile Flatela is a Professional Planner. He holds a Master Degree in Urban and Regional Planning. He is registered with the South African Council for Planners, Pr. Pl A/063/2008.
He has also been involved in land survey, research, planning and human settlements work in the public sector (national, provincial and municipal spheres) since 1991. He is one of the two founding Directors of NFA Town and Regional Planners, practising since 2017. He has been involved in academic and research assisting Nelson Mandela University.

ABOUT ASADUMA

Asaduma Gqabaza is currently a Candidate Town Planner with 3 years of professional experience in Urban and Regional Planning. She holds an Advanced Diploma in Urban and Regional Planning from the Cape Peninsula University of Technology (CPUT), where she developed a strong foundation in land-use management, sustainable development, and spatial planning.
