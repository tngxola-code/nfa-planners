about NFA Town and Regional Planners

We are a well-established consultancy company specialising in land use and spatial planning solutions.

NFA Town and Regional Planners is a leading provider of spatial solutions, established in 2016.
TWith extensive experience in Town and Regional Planning, Land Surveying, GIS, and other related fields, we deliver expert services to clients across the built environment.
As a socially responsible company, we are committed to empowering previously disadvantaged individuals through various development initiatives. These include:
  • Comprehensive on-the-job training for unskilled personnel
  • In-service training for recently qualified graduates
  • Financial assistance for students pursuing studies in Town and Regional Planning, Land Surveying, and GIS
By investing in the growth and development of our people, we contribute to a more equitable and sustainable future for all.

Town Planning

Comprehensive land use and spatial planning services, including land development planning, development guidelines, urban design, regional planning strategies, Investment/Precinct planning and management and rural and coastal development planning.

Land Surveying

Precise land measurement, engineering surveys, topographic surveys, drone mapping, and powerline surveys.

Geographic Information Systems (GIS)

Advanced GIS services for spatial data analysis, mapping, and decision-making support.
